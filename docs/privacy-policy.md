# webcraft Firefox extension — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-21

## Summary

webcraft Integration Module is the browser companion to the webcraft desktop
app. It sends downloads to the desktop app running on your own computer. The
developer does not receive your data and operates no analytics, advertising,
or remote processing service.

## Data the extension handles

- Download URLs, filenames, MIME types, and sizes, plus page URL and title.
- Cookies for the page being downloaded, sent only to the webcraft desktop
  app on your own computer via Firefox native messaging, so downloads that
  require a logged-in session can work.
- Media stream URLs (playlists and media files) detected on pages you visit.

## Data transmitted off your device

None to the developer. Extension data leaves Firefox only through native
messaging to the locally installed webcraft desktop app on the same
computer. No servers, analytics, tracking, or third-party sharing.

## Storage

The extension stores nothing persistently. Detected media candidates are
kept in memory only and cleared when you navigate or close the tab.

## Desktop app

Downloads accepted by webcraft are handled under the desktop app's own
behavior on your computer.
